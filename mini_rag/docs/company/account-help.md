# Account Help

To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link is emailed to you and expires after 30 minutes.

To change your email address, open Settings, then Profile, then Email. You must confirm the new address by clicking a link we send there.

Accounts that are inactive for 24 months are deleted automatically after a warning email.

To delete your account now, contact support from the email address on the account.
