# Refund Policy (current, effective 2026-01-01)

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

Digital products are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of renewal. After 14 days no refund is given.
