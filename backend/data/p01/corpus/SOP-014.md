# SOP-014: Reefer Unit Alarm Response

1. Any staff member on site must acknowledge a reefer temperature alarm within 10 minutes. Silencing an alarm without investigating it is prohibited.
2. After acknowledging the alarm, check the unit's power supply and the backup generator.
3. If the unit cannot be restored within 15 minutes, call the contracted refrigeration vendor using the emergency dispatch line and record the time of the call in TempTrack.
4. Move product to a functioning unit if one with spare capacity is available.
5. Notify the depot supervisor immediately.

All night-shift staff, including security guards, must complete SOP-014 training before working unsupervised shifts.
