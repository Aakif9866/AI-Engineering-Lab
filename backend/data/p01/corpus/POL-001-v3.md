# Cold Chain Storage Policy, version 3

This policy applies to every Meridian Logistics depot that stores temperature-sensitive biologics.

## Storage range
All refrigerated biologics must be stored between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius at all times. Sensors in every reefer unit record the temperature once per minute into TempTrack.

## Excursions
A temperature excursion is any period during which a product is above 8 degrees Celsius or below 2 degrees Celsius. Any excursion lasting longer than 30 minutes requires the affected lot to be quarantined immediately. The depot supervisor must notify Quality Assurance within 4 hours of detecting the excursion.

## Disposition
A quarantined lot may only be released or destroyed with written sign-off from the QA Director. A release decision must cite supporting stability data for the specific product family.

## Changes from version 2
Version 3 shortens the quarantine trigger from 60 minutes to 30 minutes and the QA notification window from 24 hours to 4 hours. Version 2 is superseded.
