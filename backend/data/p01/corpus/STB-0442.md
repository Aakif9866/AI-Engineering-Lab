# Stability Study STB-0442

Objective: determine potency retention of VX-88 family vaccines after short temperature excursions.

Method: three lots were held at 12, 15 and 20 degrees Celsius for 1, 2 and 4 hours, then assayed for potency against reference standard.

Results: full potency (at least 98 percent of reference) was retained after up to 2 hours at temperatures up to 15 degrees Celsius. Exposure at 20 degrees Celsius for 2 hours or longer reduced potency to 91 percent, which is below the release specification of 95 percent.

Conclusion: excursions of up to 2 hours at or below 15 degrees Celsius do not affect product quality.
