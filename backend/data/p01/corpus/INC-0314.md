# Incident Report INC-0314

Location: Depot 7, Reno. Equipment: reefer unit R-17.

At 02:10 on 14 March 2026 the R-17 temperature alarm was acknowledged by the night shift. The unit's compressor had stopped. The temperature inside R-17 peaked at 11.4 degrees Celsius. TempTrack shows the excursion lasted 52 minutes in total.

The affected product was lot VX-88213, containing 1,200 vials of the VX-88 vaccine. The lot was moved to unit R-12 and placed in quarantine at 03:05. QA was notified at 05:30.

FrostLine was called and a technician arrived on site at 07:40. The technician found a failed compressor relay and replaced it.

Root cause is under investigation.
