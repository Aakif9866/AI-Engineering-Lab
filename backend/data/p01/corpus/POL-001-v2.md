# Cold Chain Storage Policy, version 2

Refrigerated biologics must be stored between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

An excursion lasting longer than 60 minutes requires the affected lot to be quarantined. The depot supervisor must notify Quality Assurance within 24 hours.

Disposal of a quarantined lot requires sign-off from the depot manager.
