# Service Agreement CTR-2291

Parties: Meridian Logistics and FrostLine Refrigeration Services.

Covered equipment: FL-450X reefer units at Depots 3, 5 and 7, including unit R-17 at Depot 7.

## Emergency response
FrostLine must have a qualified technician on site within 4 hours of receiving an emergency call. The response clock starts at the time Meridian places the call to the FrostLine emergency dispatch line, as recorded in Meridian's telephone system.

## Penalties
For every hour, or part of an hour, by which the on-site response exceeds 4 hours, FrostLine pays a service credit of 2,500 US dollars.

## Maintenance
FrostLine performs preventive maintenance on every covered unit quarterly and replaces faulty sensors within 14 days of a work order.
