# Disposition of lot VX-88213

Lot VX-88213 was exposed to a maximum of 11.4 degrees Celsius for 52 minutes during incident INC-0314.

Stability study STB-0442 shows that products in the VX-88 family maintain full potency after up to 2 hours at temperatures up to 15 degrees Celsius. The exposure is within the validated range.

Decision: lot VX-88213 is released from quarantine for distribution, effective 19 March 2026. Signed, Priya Raman, QA Director.

Note: QA was notified at 05:30, which is outside the 4-hour notification window required by POL-001 version 3. This is recorded as a separate deviation, DEV-0091.
